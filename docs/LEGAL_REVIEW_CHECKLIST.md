# Legal and privacy review checklist

Qualified counsel and the privacy owner must confirm the following against the deployed product, signed vendor terms, and target jurisdictions:

- Correct legal entity, registered address, governing law, dispute process, minimum age, canonical domain, and monitored contacts.
- Data inventory, purposes, legal bases, model processing, payment/authentication flows, international transfers, cookies/analytics, support access, and subprocessors match reality.
- Message, task, goal, generated-output, provider telemetry, billing, audit, deletion-job, tombstone, and backup retention periods are defensible and operationally enforced.
- Consent/acceptance records and versioning are adequate; material policy changes have an approved re-consent or notice process.
- Access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, identity verification, appeals, and statutory response deadlines are documented and tested.
- Subscription renewal, cancellation, refunds, tax, pricing, trials, availability disclaimers, AI limitations, liability, and prohibited-use terms match the offered plan and jurisdiction.
- Processor DPAs, security commitments, breach-notification terms, deletion/return terms, and transfer mechanisms are signed and reflected on `/subprocessors`.
- Security reporting and incident notification processes have named owners and do not promise controls the organization does not operate.

Record counsel name/firm, jurisdictions reviewed, date, policy versions, approved changes, unresolved risks, and next review date. Re-review before entering a new jurisdiction or materially changing data, AI, billing, analytics, or subprocessors.
